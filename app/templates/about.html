How to read the numbers

  • Real growth is adjusted for price changes. Sector growth uses chain-linked volume indices, so inflation is not counted as growth.
  • The real GDP index (2019 = 100) is chained from yearly real growth rates of the World Bank.
  • Quarterly and monthly German series are seasonally and calendar adjusted; changes compare a period with the same period a year earlier.
  • Forecasts (IMF) are marked as such in the tile label and come from the World Economic Outlook.
  • Published figures are revised. Each data refresh replaces the stored series with the latest revision.
  • Inflation (HICP) for Europe follows the 2026 switch of Eurostat to the ECOICOP 2 classification.
